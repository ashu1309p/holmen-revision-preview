Start a company in Denmark

For you if you live abroad or have just moved to Denmark and want to start a business. We help you from the first meeting to your first VAT return.

Book a free meeting

For you if …

  • want to start an ApS or a sole proprietorship in Denmark
  • have a company abroad and want to set up in Denmark
  • want bookkeeping, VAT and tax in order from day one

How it works

  1. 1

    Free meeting

    We talk about your plans and your situation.

  2. 2

    Company type

    We recommend the company type that suits you.

  3. 3

    Incorporation and registration

    We help you incorporate and register the company.

  4. 4

    Setup

    We set up bookkeeping and the registrations you need.

  5. 5

    Ongoing help

    We handle bookkeeping, VAT and accounts once you are up and running.

What you need

  • A valid passport or other ID
  • Details of the owners and management
  • A short description of what the business will do
